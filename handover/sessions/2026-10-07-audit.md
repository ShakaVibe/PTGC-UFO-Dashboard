## What Audit III says in one paragraph

The dashboard is in good shape where the last month's work went: every redesigned surface is honest about what it does not know,
accessible names and roles are there, the pipeline is really hourly and every step fails safe. The trouble is at three edges.
(1) **Speed**: every visitor waits ~15 s of Babel compile that a one-attribute change cuts to ~3 s (c1), and nothing — no data
request, no header art — starts until that compile ends (c14); the Tailwind compiler, the `?t=` cache-busters and 6.5 MB of q90
JPGs are the rest of the bill (c12, c13, c15). (2) **The seams the redesign did not reach**: the PTGC page scrolls sideways on
every phone and the tab band lost its scroll cue when v2 went live (c2, c3); the Affiliates page, the disclaimer, the Logos
modals, the Targets card, the phone LP rows and the four sibling-page bodies are the 2025 look under the new header (c32–c36);
and a dozen small honesty regressions crept into the new code (c5, c6, c23). (3) **Things that will break on a date**: the three
CoinGecko history files are capped at 500 points, which at 24 runs a day is 21 days — the "30D" sparklines lose their window
around Oct 20 (c4); every staleness threshold is still sized for 5 runs a day (c17). The product ideas (c50–c65) all build on
files the pipeline already writes — a "since yesterday" strip, a Telegram digest, a time-machine calculator, pool-health and
fee-history trends — and are where the next month's visits come from.
