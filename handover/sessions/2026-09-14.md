## What changed (`index.html` only, +~90 lines; not yet committed at the time of writing)
- **`DaoCreatures`** (module scope, right after `DaoBuysStat`): a PTGC amount as Grays
  creatures. Reuses `getBurnC(pct)` — the same greedy change-making the burn tiles use
  (🐋 = 1 % of starting supply, 🦈 0.1 %, 🐬 0.01 %, 🦑 0.001 %, 🐢 0.0001 %, 🦐 0.00001 %),
  `.slice(0,3)` = top three tiers (same cut as the KPI page's lifetime-burn line). Counts ≤ 4
  are drawn as a row of creatures (`e.repeat(cnt)`), 5+ as `🦈×12`. `role="img"` with an
  aria-label in words ("2 Whales, 5 Dolphins, 9 Squids"). Below a shrimp → a single 🐚.
  Lifetime 6.87 B = 🐋🐋 🐬×5 🦑×9 (checked by hand: 2.0599 % of supply).
- **`DaoBuysStat`** gained an optional `foot` prop (centred flex row under `sub`, may wrap).
  The "PTGC bought" tile passes `<DaoCreatures ptgc={view.t.ptgc} size="text-lg lg:text-base"/>`
  — one line in the 7-column desktop grid, larger in the 2-column phone grid. Skeleton tile 0
  got a matching extra `Sk` line (gotcha 11).
- **Recent-buys ledger**: new box after the chart/cadence container, before the footer, same
  `mx-3 sm:mx-6 rounded-2xl bg-[#0b0b0b]` shell as the chart. `ledgerN` state (10, +10 per
  "Show 10 more"); rows = `view.buysAll` (LIFETIME, newest first — deliberately not the
  selected range; fresh on-chain buys are included and carry the NEW pill). Value = `b.usd`
  (what was paid at the block's PLS/USD). Date links to the PulseScan tx.
  - sm+: `<table>` Date · time ↗ | PTGC bought | PLS spent | Value | Grays (Grays th is

  - phones (`sm:hidden`): a `<ul>` with two lines per buy — date · time ↗ / `43.88M PTGC`,
    then `200.00M PLS ($2,224)` / creatures right-aligned. The 5-column table was too

  - Header row: "RECENT BUYS" + "10 of 211".
