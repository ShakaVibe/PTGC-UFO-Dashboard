# 2026-09-28 — UFO/WETH out of the RH Cores liquidity modal

Shaka: "when hitting the little RH icon in the liquidity box on the dashboard, UFO has WETH in
there, that is not one of the RH cores. The RH Cores in the social is correct."

## Cause

`HARDCODED_UFO_PAIRS` (top of the script) seeds the four constructor pools and flagged the WETH one
`isRHCore: true`; `resolveUfoPairs` then stamped `isRHCore:true` on every pool it re-confirmed from
the factory (WPLS, PLSX, WETH). When DexScreener does not return a seeded pool, `fetchDex` reads it
from chain and carries `_rhCore: hcPair.isRHCore ? quoteSymbol : null`, and both `filteredSorted`
(the RH-only switch on the pairs table) and `rhCoresPairs` (the RH modal + its total) take the
`_rhCore` placeholder BEFORE the `RH_CORES` symbol check — so WETH walked straight in. The tell in
Shaka's screenshot: UFO/WETH at `$0` volume / `0.0%`, i.e. a chain-read row. The Socials card was
right because it builds from the `RH_CORES` list (`getPairData(pairs, core)`), never from the flag.

## Fix (index.html only, 3 lines)

- `HARDCODED_UFO_PAIRS`: WETH `isRHCore: false` (comment says why).
- `resolveUfoPairs`: `partners` carries the seed's `isRHCore`; the returned pair uses it instead
  of a hard `true`. `bootstrapUFO` copies only `.address` onto the seed, so the flag was never
  overwritten from there — but a pool the factory knows and the seed does not would still be
  pushed whole, and now arrives with the right flag.

Not touched: `calculators.html` keeps WETH in `MOON_CORE_TOKENS` on purpose (Moon Math's indirect
model wants every pool, and its own `_rhCore` is never read); its L/V "RH Cores Only" is
address-based (`LV_RH_CORE_SET`) and never included WETH.
