## 1. What is true now (verified on chain)

1. **PTGC: 5 % per transfer.** Every taxed transfer emits, from the same sender in the same tx: 0.5 % → `0x…0369` (a direct burn —
   nothing is bought), 1 % → `0xc71f…39ef` (the PTGC **staking contract**, = the site's "Stakers"), 2.5 % → the PTGC contract
   (presumably the site's DAO 2 % + Add LP 0.5 %, NOT yet traced — v37), and ~1 % that emits no Transfer (reflections = "Holders",
   inferred). Fingerprint used here: burn leg × 2 = staking leg in the same tx from the same sender (exact to 1e-6).
2. **UFO: ~6 % per transfer** — 5 % visibly to the UFO contract, ~1 % reflections. UFO is **not renounced** (owner `0x92dd8e86…`);
   its rates can change (the site already reads them live on index, not on calculators — v30).
3. **Bot loops pay once.** Side pool pays PTGC straight into PTGC/WPLS: 1 transfer, 1 fee, 2 Swap events. Bot txs: taxed amount ÷
   PTGC swap-leg amount = **0.49** (7 d). 24 h: 1,351 bot txs = 2,271 swap legs (906 two-leg loops, 439 single tracked leg — mostly
   dust, median $0.09 — 6 longer). (The 906 quoted in the chat on Oct 25 is `arb.txs`, the round trips only.)
4. **Router splits make people pay twice.** The PulseX app's smart router (PulseXSwapRouter `0xda9aba4e…`, verified; it is NOT a
   bot) splits a buy across pools; each pool pays the ROUTER (taxed), the router pays the buyer (taxed again) → ~10 % on a buy.
   Receipt of tx `0x42c50f41…`: four pools → router, each with its 0.5 / 1 / 2.5 % legs, then router → buyer 207.1 M with its own
   legs. Human txs over 7 d: taxed ÷ swap legs = **1.26** (PulseXSwapRouter 1.36 avg, ~1.95 on big split buys; switch.win adapters
   1.03–1.12; PulseX v1 router 0.97).
5. **Wallet → wallet sends, staking moves, untracked pools** also pay the fee and appear in no volume ("transfers" below).
6. **The staking contract burns.** `0xc71f…` calls `0xb1fa319a` on claims and sends part of the payout to `0x…0369` (fee-exempt, no
   legs): 1,470 transfers / 65.4 M PTGC in 30 d (one 28.8 M tx in the last 7 d). So part of "Stakers 1 %" later shows up as burned.
7. **UFO's own swapbacks** (the contract selling its fee UFO, buying + burning UFO and PTGC, adding LP) go through the PulseX v2
   router and are classed HUMAN by build-swap-volume (856 legs in 90 d; 5–8 % of UFO "human" USD). They pay no UFO fee (exempt).
