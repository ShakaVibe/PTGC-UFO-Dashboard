   `h2Alpha`) as the whole glow, and a fill of `.16` under the line → `.04` at 65 % → 0. Y: min → 55, max → 6 of the
   60 u box (per series, 6 u air on top, 5 u below). Straight segments between the real samples — nothing smoothed.

6. **Sparklines, third pass — layered light, same geometry** (Shaka: "much closer"; then a second spec: dynamic
   luminosity like the reference, nothing thicker, no size/position/data/layout change). `H2Spark` now draws, bottom
   to top: the .16 → 0 fill; **pools of light** under the strongest peaks (a radial gradient `<ellipse rx=20 ry=14>`
   at each chosen peak, CLIPPED to the area so it only bleeds downward and never reads as a dot); a 4.5 px halo at
   .12; the **2 px base line** in the colour (its 2 px `drop-shadow` at .5 alpha is the soft glow); a 3 px bloom in
   the lighter tint over ±14 x-units of each chosen peak; a **.9 px lighter core** (`h2Tint(color,.55)`) along the
   whole line; and a **1 px near-white core** (`h2Tint(color,.88)`) over ±6 x-units of each chosen peak, opacity
   .5 + .5·k. **`h2Peaks(vs,xs)`** picks the peaks: local maxima with prominence ≥ 12 % of the range (an endpoint

   are within 12 x-units, at most 4, k = (prominence / strongest)^1.5 — the strongest is near-white, the rest
   fall off, most peaks get nothing. Nothing about the line's position, the box or the tiles changed.
