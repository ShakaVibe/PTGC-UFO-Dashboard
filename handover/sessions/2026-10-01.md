## Round 3 — 30-day window, one colour per token, bigger figures

7. **Sparklines are 30 days now, every tile** (`H2_DAYS=30`, next to `H2_TTL`; Shaka asked my opinion and took it).
   Why: the pipeline's ~5 runs/day gave a 7-day line 21 samples (the angular look); a month is ~104–182 samples at the
   live box width, and the slow metrics (Holders, Tokens in LP, Liquidity) get a shape instead of a step. Market Cap
   asks GeckoTerminal for `limit=${H2_DAYS*24}` = 720 hourly candles (one call; the API's cap is 1000); `since` in
   `fetchH2Price7d` (name kept) and `useH2Series` both read `H2_DAYS`; the aria-labels print it. The 24h figures
   and Volume's "vs 7d avg" are untouched — the number says today, the line says the month. **A tiny "30D" tag**
   (`.h2-spw`, bottom-right of each tile, `rgba(255,255,255,.32)`, 12 u / 8 u on phones) names the window.
   Known and accepted: a pipeline stall shows as a straight run between two samples; over a month there are a few.
8. **One line colour per token:** gold `#FFD24D` on PTGC, green `#8DFF3A` on UFO, on all seven tiles — the
   `color:` extras on LIQ / MCAP (lime) and TXNS (green) are gone, so a green line on this page means UFO, never
   "up", and a red ▼ 24h beside a green line cannot be misread. (Shaka raised the ▼-red / line-green question; I
   argued against up/down line colours — the arrow and percentage carry direction, the line is the brand art.)
9. **Bigger figures:** `.h2-tv` 38 → 46 u (`small` 22 → 26 u), top 68 → 66 u; the change line moves 116 → 120 u
   (ends ~149 u, chart box starts at 160 u). Phone: 23 → 27 u (`small` 14 → 16 u), top 43 u, change line 74 u.
   Skeleton bar matched (46 u). **`H2Val`** (module scope, above `H2Tri`) wraps the figure and shrinks it to the tile
   when it would overrun: `useLayoutEffect` after every render + on resize, measuring with a `Range`'s fractional
   width (scrollWidth/clientWidth are whole px — at 1024 "$42,600,000" overran by .4 px, measured as "fits", and the
   ellipsis had eaten two digits). A number is never truncated: at 1024 it reads 21.42 px instead of 21.75.

Checked (harness, `index.orig.html` = `22f6c28ed`): PTGC 1440, UFO 1024, PTGC 390 — figures fit, "30D" tag in every
