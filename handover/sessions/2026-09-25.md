## What shipped (index.html + two images)
- `HOME_DESIGN` (next to `UFO_MAINTENANCE`): `'cosmic'` now, `'classic'` = the design before today.
  `homeDesign()` also honours `?home=classic` / `?home=cosmic` in the URL, so either can be looked at on
  the live site without a deploy (e.g. `https://ptgc-ufo.com/?home=classic`).
- `Home` is unchanged except for one early `return` when the design is cosmic. All state, loaders,
  the a62 per-card paint, the a51 retry ladder, the 5-minute refresh, `SwapModal`, the maintenance
  notice and the secret dot are shared; only the view differs. The classic JSX below it is untouched.
- New module-scope components just above `Home`: `HomeCosmicView`, `CosmicCard`, `CosmicCardSkeleton`,
  `HomeSpark` (+ `fetchHomeSpark`, `homeSparkFallback`, `HomeSparkFlat`, `HcSk`). Styles: the `hc-*`
  block at the end of the `<style>` head (plain CSS, not Tailwind classes, so the harness CSS build and
  the play CDN cannot disagree about them).
- Art: `logos/home/cosmic-bg.jpg` (Shaka's image with its baked-in title and cards painted out and the
  card area darkened into a soft vignette, 1536x1024, 204 KB) and `logos/home/cosmic-ground.jpg` (the
  landscape strip for the foot of the phone layout). They live under `logos/` because `deploy.yml`'s
  `_site` allow-list copies `logos/` whole — a new top-level folder would NOT be published (a38).
- Layout: `--bgw` = clamp(1000px, 100vw, 1920px) is the painted width of the art; the title's top
  (0.172 x) and the card band follow it, so the cards sit on the dark part of the art at every desktop
  width. Two cards side by side from 1024px, stacked below; below 1024px the art fades out under the
  title and the ground strip returns at the bottom. Past 1920px the art stays 1920 wide and fades into
  the page colour at its sides.
- Card contents = the classic card's: price (`Price`, so the a48 sr-only full decimal is kept), 24 h
  change, contract + copy, Day counter, MCap (abbreviated below 640px), 24 h volume, PLS ratio, the
  chart, BUY / SELL, and "DASHBOARD ›" (a real button; the whole card still opens the dashboard).
  Null data prints "—" everywhere (gotcha 20); the change chip is "—", not a green 0.00%.
- **The chart is real data.** GeckoTerminal 15-minute closes for the token's `mainPair` with
  `token=<address>` (verified in a browser 2026-09-25: PTGC side, ~$0.0000388). GeckoTerminal skips
  intervals with no trades, so a quiet token has fewer points — drawn where they fall, never filled in.
  The series must end within 25% of the card's DexScreener price or it is not used. Fallback: the
  classic sparkline's five back-computed points; with neither, a flat dashed line. One request per
  card, cached 10 minutes. charts.html already depends on the same API.
