## Later: the KPI tiles' sparklines (Shaka's screenshot — Market Cap + Liq/MCap dashed for ~5 s, then drawn)

Those two are the GeckoTerminal series (`fetchH2Price7d`, 720 hourly candles); the other five come from the pipeline files.
Two things were wrong, both v2-only, both in `tools/sibling-header.py` (last section): (1) a series that had not landed was
`null` after the FIRST miss, and `H2Spark` draws null as the dashed "history unavailable" line — so the tile claimed
"unavailable" while the retry was still running. Now `useH2Series` keeps a missing series `undefined` while retries remain
(`keep(fresh,old)`), `H2Spark` draws `undefined` as a solid, breathing baseline (`.h2-spwait`, h2.css) and the dashed line only
after the last attempt (gotcha 20's spirit: a claim the page can back). (2) the candles lived only in a module cache, so every
reload / token switch waited again — `LS.H2_PX` (`grays_h2_px_v1`, 30 min, `H2_PX_TTL`) now holds them; a reload paints at
once. Harness: `probes/h2-spark-state.js`; the stub finally answers the tiles' candle call (it had been falling into the
per-pool branch, whose flat 1e-4 close failed the 25 % sanity check — Market Cap never drew in the harness since 2026-10-01).
Proven: slow (6 s) → wait → drawn → reload drawn at 0.6 s; 404 → wait through the retries → DASHED at ~48 s; flaky → drawn.
Classic fingerprint unchanged.
