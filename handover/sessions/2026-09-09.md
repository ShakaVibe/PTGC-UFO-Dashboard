## Delivered — one commit, `index.html` only (+151 / −79)

### u1 — one `Modal` for every overlay
- New **MODAL SHELL** block just above the SHARE CARD SHELL: `MODAL_STACK`, `claimKeys()`,
  `useDialog(dlgRef,onClose)`, `Modal`. The 16 hand-rolled overlays (Live Feed, Holder analytics,
  Maintenance notice, Holder tiers, both KPI reports, RH LP, Volume, the three Logos modals,
  Referrer details ×2, Affiliate leaderboard, Payment receipts, Disclaimer) are now
  `<Modal className="…" onClose={…} label="…">` with the same classes they had, minus
  `fixed inset-0`. Panels inside are untouched (they still `stopPropagation`).
- What every modal now does: `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal` + an `aria-label`; portalled to
  `<body>`; Escape closes the **topmost** layer only; focus moves in on open, Tab/Shift+Tab cycle
  inside, focus returns to the opener on close (or to the dialog underneath if the opener
  unmounted); body scroll locked while any modal is open (ref-counted); backdrop click closes and
  no longer bubbles to an outer modal.
- `ShareCardModal` now uses `useDialog` instead of its own copy of the effect.
- `InfoTip` claims the same key stack while its popover is open, so Escape inside a modal closes
  the ⓘ popover first and the modal on the next press.
- The Disclaimer passes no `onClose`: Escape/backdrop do nothing, Tab stays inside.

### u9 — accessibility
- `aria-label` on 19 icon-only buttons the scan found: 12 modal close ✕/×, 4 address-copy buttons,
  the KPI 📷 share button, the Affiliates back chevron, the pool-details ℹ️ (also `aria-expanded`).
  Period pills (`{p}`, `{label}`) are text and were left alone.
- Tab strips: both nav rows are `<nav aria-label="Dashboard sections">`; the three tab buttons
  carry `aria-current="page"` when active (desktop + mobile).
- Focus ring: one `:focus-visible` rule for button/a/input/select/textarea/[tabindex] (dialog
  containers excluded); nav items use `outline-offset:-2px` because the strips clip vertically.
- Reduced motion: the existing rule now also stops `animate-pulse`, the u12 sweep, the buy-button
  shimmer, and the hover transforms/transitions on cards. Spinners keep turning on purpose.
