## The discussion (what was decided, and why)

- **Feasible, and fast, ONLY if the chain work is in the pipeline.** Probed first: rpc.pulsechain.com serves archive state
  (balanceOf at a block 30 days back answers in ~140 ms) and a week of PTGC transfers is ~35,000 events (30 days ~187,000)
  — 15–60 s in a browser, throttled on busy days; ~2 min once in the pipeline, then one small file per hour.
- **Definition (Shaka picked, recommended):** a wallet is NEW when its balance went 0 → above 0 in the window; it LEFT when it
  went back to 0. A wallet that comes back after leaving shows as new again with a "back" badge. Not "first-ever holders".
- **Source: the pipeline file, instant load** (not a live top-up — he picked the plain file).
- **Exclusions:** contracts (pools, routers, staking, the DAO contract, the burn address, the token contracts) and the two DAO
  treasury wallets — "only real wallets".
- **"It needs to match what the dashboard says."** The crux. The Holders box's 24H change is PulseScan's count today minus
  its count at the daily snapshot (`getHolderChange`), i.e. NET — arrivals minus departures, pools included. There is no list
  of "the five" behind a "+5"; if 12 arrived and 7 left it reads +5. Explained; Shaka chose: the window lists the arrivals AND
  the departures and prints the net, and the Holders box's change line reads the same count once it is live — one count,
  two surfaces. Until go-live the box keeps PulseScan's figure (nothing visible changes before he flips).
- Per dashboard with a PTGC / UFO switch inside; newest first; no share card for now.
