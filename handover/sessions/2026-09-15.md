## "Market then" vs "Price paid" (question from the Spaces)
Checked against `scripts/build-dao-buys.mjs`: `price` = PLS spent × PLS/USD ÷ PTGC received
(all-in: 5% fee + slippage); `marketUsd` = PTGC/WPLS pair reserves × WPLS/DAI reserves read at
the buy's block (`pricesAt`), i.e. the pool's spot price, no fee/slippage. Dots sit on market;
Spent / Avg cost / dashed line use paid. Community post drafted for Shaka.

## Bug: "PTG" on a YouTuber's stream (the Buy/Sell modal header)
Screenshot showed `SWAP ON SWITC…` and `PTG` — the eyebrow truncated and the title's last
glyph gone. Reproduced in the harness with
`eval=document.documentElement.style.fontSize='20px'` (what Chrome's Appearance → Font size
"Large" does): every rem in the header grows (logo 80→100px, gaps, 36→45px Orbitron title) but
the panel was capped at `sm:max-w-[460px]`, so the title column shrank below the word. The
overflowing "C" was invisible rather than overlapping because `.metallic-gold` is
`background-clip:text` + transparent text — glyphs outside the element box get no gradient.

Fix: `sm:max-w-[460px]` → `sm:max-w-[28.75rem]` (identical at 16px; scales with the viewer's
font setting), with a comment above the panel line in `SwapModal`. Verified at 16/20/24px root
on 1440×900 and 20px at 375×812: full "PTGC", eyebrow untruncated, default layout unchanged.
Harness ran in the cloud workspace (`CHROME=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome`).

Not changed: the widget box height (`min(720px, 100dvh − 300px)`) is px on purpose — the panel
scrolls rather than clips when the header is taller. The header's `w-[420px] h-[220px]` glow is
cosmetic.
