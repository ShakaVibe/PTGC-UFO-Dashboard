10 Yr Projection stops counting burns as buying

The fee is taken in tokens. PTGC's 0.5% is burned straight from each transfer, so no PLS ever buys anything. UFO's contract sells its fee tokens first to pay for its burns and liquidity. Only the DAO line really buys.

Costs and amounts include the fee

The fee comes off the transfer into your wallet. To end up with N more tokens you buy N ÷ 0.95. A sell of N tokens only gets PLS for 95% of them. A buy the PulseX app splits across pools pays the fee twice.
