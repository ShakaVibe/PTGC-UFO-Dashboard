D — HOW REFLECTIONS WORK
No numbers, just the explanation people ask for: the 1%, who gets it, why there is nothing to claim, why burned / pool / excluded balances don’t count. Static, zero reads, always the same height as Lock positions. The least interesting but the safest.
ⓘ How UFO reflections workthe 1% holder reward, explainedHide ▴

Every UFO buy, sell and transfer pays a 6% fee. 1% of it is shared among all holders in proportion to their balance — it simply appears in your wallet, there is nothing to claim.

Burned tokens, the liquidity pools and a few excluded wallets don’t take part, so your share is measured against the ≈734B UFO that does.

The page works out what you’ve earned from your transfer history: balance now minus everything you received plus everything you sent.
