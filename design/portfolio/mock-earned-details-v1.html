2 · THE WINDOW — PTGC
At the top: the 30-day total, then one tile per source with its total, dollars and daily average (the thin colour line under each tile is its share). The chart has one stacked bar per day, staking + locking + reflections. Hover a bar for that day's breakdown (shown here on one day), and click a legend chip to hide a source. Below the chart is the day-by-day list, newest first, with the best day marked. PTGC / UFO tabs sit top right.
3 · THE WINDOW — UFO TAB
UFO has one source, so one green bar per day, with tiles for the 30-day total, the daily average and the best day.
4 · ON A PHONE
The window becomes a full-height sheet: tabs across the top, the total on top and the three sources in a row, a shorter chart, and the day list as small cards, each with a mini split bar.
