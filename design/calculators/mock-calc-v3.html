Educational Tool — For Fun Only

Moon Math is an educational entertainment tool designed to help you visualize market mechanics, liquidity dynamics, and price reflexivity. It uses two models: Direct Buy simulates buying PTGC against its total aggregated DEX liquidity using constant product (x × y = k). Indirect (Core) simulates buying an RH Core token — the core's price rises, but PTGC's gain is dampened by pool weight: since the PTGC/Core pool is only a fraction of PTGC's total liquidity, arbitrage bots equalize PTGC's price across all pools, limiting the net impact.

This is NOT: a price predictor, financial advisor, guarantee of future returns, or substitute for your own research.

There are SO MANY real-world factors that can change these numbers including: arbitrage bots, MEV/front-running, multi-hop routing, slippage, sell pressure, market sentiment, liquidity additions/removals, token burns, whale activity, exchange listings, regulatory changes, macro conditions, and overall crypto market cycles. Pool ratios do not remain constant and can shift dramatically.
