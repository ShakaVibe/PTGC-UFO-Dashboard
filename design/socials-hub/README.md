## Decisions

- **Background**: `logos/socials/hub-sky.jpg` (his 1312 × 1199 scene), used once, `cover`, anchored top; never mirrored or
  repeated ("ours has too many big planets"). A black gap under the tab band and a fade from black at the top so it never runs
  up into the bar. Phones: scene top under the title, its planet at the foot (`img.ft`).
- **Title**: "Socials" gold metallic + "Hub" silver (Orbitron 900, 64 px / 44 px on phones), "Shareable Cards & Images for the
  Community", "Logos • Stats • Charts • Reports • And More"; a soft dark radial pool behind the block for legibility.
- **Columns**: gold PTGC / green UFO / blue Combined, 2 px border, dark glass; header = 96 px logo, name (Orbitron 900 27 px,
  Combined's in white), "PulseChain / The Grays" ("PTGC + UFO / The Grays"), a decorative circled chevron; a darker pool behind
  the header on all three.
- **Rows**: 62 px, icon 70 × 56 left, title 17.5 px in the row's colour family (gold / fire / green / lime / purple / blue / red),
  sub-line 13.5 px white, chevron right. Logos rows carry the token logos themselves; RH Core Liquidity the dashboard's droplet;
  The Grays & The Cores his gold globe (60 px, gold glow).
- **Order**: Logos, Burn Stats, Value Generated, KPI Report, Holders (every column, level) → Token Allocation, Targets (PTGC + UFO;
  Combined: RH Core Liquidity, Leagues) → each column's own (DAO Treasury, DAO Buys, Affiliates Report / PTGC Burned by UFO /
  The Grays & The Cores).
- **Icons** are provisional cuts from his mock-up image (`logos/socials/hub/<col>-<key>.png`); the full-size sheet is owed and
  swaps in file-for-file.
